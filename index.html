Our story / Established 1997

A practice shaped
by understanding.

Led by Yoga A Yogaparan, KVT 3 Architects brings architectural design and town planning into one conversation. Our work considers the complete picture: from the surrounding context to the detail of an individual space.

Our approach values functionality, energy efficiency, thoughtful use of materials and lasting value. Each project is a dialogue, shaped around the client’s needs and the qualities of its location.

03 / Our expertise

Creative thinking.
Practical direction.

Architecture and town planning work best together. We help clients explore what is possible and navigate the decisions that shape their development.

Discuss your requirements ↗
01

Architecture

Site-responsive design that balances the client’s brief with function, context, energy efficiency and long-term value.

  • Single homes and multi-dwelling developments
  • Mixed-use, commercial and industrial buildings
  • Medical centres
  • Religious buildings and cultural centres
02

Town planning

Planning advice and coordination informed by architectural thinking, with experience working with councils, authorities and specialist consultants.

  • Development and subdivision proposals
  • Land-use planning and heritage matters
  • Restrictive covenants
  • Liquor licensing and advertising signs
03

Feasibility & advice

A clearer view of a property’s possibilities, with practical consideration of site constraints and development options.

  • Land-use feasibility investigations
  • Pre-purchase inspections
  • Site and contextual considerations
  • Coordination with specialist consultants
04

VCAT & planning support

Experience with VCAT hearings, compulsory conferences and expert-witness work, alongside liaison with the relevant project professionals.

Contact the practice to discuss the support appropriate to your matter.
